Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What this app is

YT Automation is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to update the owner's own Google Sheets.

Google data it uses

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): to upload videos and thumbnails, set their title, description, tags and publish time, and read the channel's own upload list to avoid uploading a video twice.
  • Google Sheets: to write the upload status and video link into the owner's own spreadsheets.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no other person's data is collected.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YT Automation's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the stored tokens are deleted from the owner's computer.
